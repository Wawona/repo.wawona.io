Sileo debs for jailbroken iOS (rootless and rootful) are listed at /search/?channel=deb. Filter architecture iphoneos-arm64 (rootless) or iphoneos-arm (rootful).

Sileo still uses https://repo.wawona.io/ as the APT source. That tree is not under /jailbreak/.

Termux is not jailbreak. Sideloaded Android packages: /termux/. Store wpm never reads this page.
