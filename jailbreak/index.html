Jailbreak debs for Sileo, Zebra, and Irisin (rootless and rootful) are listed at /search/?channel=deb. Filter architecture iphoneos-arm64 (rootless) or iphoneos-arm (rootful). Irisin is iOS and iPadOS 16 or later: rootless and roothide (iphoneos-arm64e). Rootful stays on Sileo or Zebra.

Sileo, Zebra, and Irisin still use https://repo.wawona.io/ as the APT source. Irisin can add it with irisin://repository/add?url=https://repo.wawona.io/. That tree is not under /jailbreak/.

Termux is not jailbreak. Sideloaded Android packages: /termux/. Store wpm never reads this page.
